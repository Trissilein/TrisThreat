# TrisThreat

**See your threat on every enemy, right on its nameplate.** TrisThreat puts a small capsule on each enemy that fights you and shows how much threat you hold, so you know when to hold back and when you are safe.

## What it does

- **Threat capsule:** the threat value as a number, in a tab that grows out of the enemy's health bar and shares its color.
- **Role-aware colors:** as a tank, green means you hold the enemy. As a damage dealer or healer, green is safe, orange is rising and red means you pulled it. Another tank or your pet holding the enemy shows blue.
- **Automatic role:** detected from your stance, form, group role or specialization, with a manual override.
- **Your placement:** move the capsule up to 100 pixels left, right, up or down, and change its size. A preview shows it on your target.
- **Easy access:** `/tristhreat` or `/tt`, the addon compartment, Titan Panel or the minimap icon (right-click hides it, the settings bring it back).

## Good to know

- World of Warcraft Retail and WoW Forever (beta, one package for both). English and German.
- Current clients treat threat numbers as secret values: the addon displays them, and colors by threat status.
- Everything stays in your game. The addon does not collect or send any data.
- What is new in each version is listed in the changelog of the file on this page.

## Install

Use the Wago or CurseForge app, WowUp, or extract the `TrisThreat` folder into `Interface/AddOns` (`_retail_` or `_forever_`).
