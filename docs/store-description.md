## See your threat on every enemy

TrisThreat shows how much threat you hold on every enemy that fights you, right on its nameplate. Built for WoW Forever.

### Features

- Blizzard's own nameplate art: a second level badge (left, right, top or bottom, or dragged anywhere), a segment in the bar's left or right end, or a thin strip above or below the bar; drag the element in the settings preview to place it
- Safe-target glow: marks the enemy each qualifying tank is targeting and holds with a clear lead while your own threat is low. Select a tank manually or let TrisThreat detect who is holding each enemy.
- Adjust glow color, width and position above the health bar. Set Solo and Group visibility separately.
- Color from green at 0 % over yellow to red at 100 %, one step per percent; for tanks mirrored, following your lead over the next player
- Automatic role detection (tank, healer, damage) with a manual override; the tooltip shows which rule decided
- Blue instead of red when another tank or your pet holds the enemy
- Color the number, the background, the filled part of the health bar, or any combination
- Optional room-making: moves the name, raid icon and crowd-control icons aside
- Settings window with a live copy of the nameplate; your target gets a test element without combat

### Quick access

- Slash commands /tt and /tristhreat
- Minimap button (right-click hides it)
- Titan Panel / LibDataBroker display showing the current mode
- Addon compartment entry
