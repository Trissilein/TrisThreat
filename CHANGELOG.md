# TrisThreat

## 0.1.0

- First version: a threat capsule attached to the health bar of every enemy nameplate that fights you, with value and fill bar. The nameplate bar itself can be tinted by threat (Platynator and Blizzard nameplates).
- Role-aware colors (tank, damage dealer, healer) with automatic role detection and a manual override; blue off-tank color when another tank or your pet holds the enemy.
- Settings window with position offsets (up to 100 pixels each way), size, value mode, filters and a preview on your target.
- Titan Panel / LibDataBroker launcher, addon compartment entry and a minimap icon that can be hidden with a right-click.
- Retail and WoW Forever (beta) in one package.
